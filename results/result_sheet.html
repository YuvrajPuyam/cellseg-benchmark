Fine-tuning Cellpose-SAM — whole-cell, 200-image subset, 20 epochs

+1.5 pts F1@0.5 (0.844 → 0.859), plus AJI+ +0.014, PQ +0.018, boundary-F1 +0.016, Dice +0.013 — at lr 1e-5. lr 5e-5 was too high (≈ flat). A small, consistent gain from just 200 labels.

Takeaways

  • Cellpose-SAM is the most robust zero-shot choice, and decisively so on whole-cell.
  • On nuclei the two are close — pick by what you weight: detection (Cellpose) vs. boundary tightness (μSAM wins boundary-F1 & Dice).
  • Fine-tuning works: a measurable whole-cell lift from a small labeled set.

Honest caveats

  • μSAM whole-cell now uses a fair RGB=[membrane, nuclear, membrane] input; the earlier 0.51 was a channel-mean-grayscale strawman, corrected here to 0.736.
  • StarDist runs at N=148 (vs 297) — the largest stable subset before its TF build core-dumps on this cluster; treat its row as indicative.
  • All metrics carry bootstrap 95% CIs. The fine-tune lift is one protocol; a val-based, multi-seed study (finetune_study.py) is the next refinement.
